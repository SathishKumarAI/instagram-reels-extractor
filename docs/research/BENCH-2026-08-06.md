## How to read this

- **One variable.** Every arm ran over the same fixed sample, the same cached
  frames and the same prompt. Only the model changed.
- **`empty` is a failure that parsed.** A variant with no claims at all is
  counted here, not averaged in as a processed reel.
- **`salvaged` means the answer came out of a reasoning trace**, not a finished
  reply — a reasoning model whose budget ran out mid-thought.
- **Agreement is not accuracy.** It measures whether two models made the same
  claim, not whether either is right. Claims are matched by content: frame
  pointers ("Frame 3 shows…") and reporting verbs are stripped first, and two
  claims quoting the same on-screen label count as one.
- **The reference arm is not ground truth.** `claude-cli` is the arm the corpus
  was built with, so every other model is described relative to it.
- **Cost is a price, not a bill.** See `docs/research/COSTS.md`.
- **Seconds are wall-clock on one busy workstation** (RTX 5070 Ti, 16GB, one
  model resident at a time). Treat them as ratios, not benchmarks.

## Why they differ

Written from 24 disagreeing claims sampled across the arms; every statement should be checkable against the tables above.

### local
1. Misses named specifics. Never states `"SadServers is recommended as prep for hands-on Linux and server debugging scenarios."`
2. Adds raw text fragments as claims: `"AS OF 2025,"`, `"anime.js"`, `"LeetCode is dead."` Grounded — these are literal overlay/transcript strings — but they are not claims.
3. Instruction following. It reads text off frames fine, then dumps strings instead of forming propositions. Summaries stay generic (`"It recommends specific resources for these new skills."`) while the extracted list is unsynthesised.

Note: on the PhD reel its `candidate_summary` and all six `only_candidate` entries are identical, word for word, to reels-vision.

### qwen3vl-8b
1. Misses caption/metadata claims. `"The reel is a paid partnership with Manus, tagged #manuspartner and #ad in the caption."`
2. Adds timestamped overlay narration: `"At 4 seconds, the text overlay 'So, I decided to reply' indicates the speaker's action of responding to the inquiry email."` Grounded — reference confirms the reply — but it reports the overlay, not the point.
3. Instruction following. Content it clearly saw appears in prose but not as claims: its leetcode summary names `"sadservers, the testing trophy, Google's code review guide, MIT's missing semester"` while all four still sit in `only_reference`.

### qwen3vl-4b
1. Misses applicant-side fine print. `"The applicant states their own background as an MBA from the University of Education, Lahore, Multan Campus, and an MS in Management Sciences."`
2. Adds inverted rule content: `"Touching the kitchen area is now considered illegal under the 2025 rules."` Invented — reference calls it a `"clarification"` about when a volley `"officially begins"`, not a ban. Same reel adds `"Pre-2025 regulations permitted shots that would be illegal today."`
3. World knowledge. It fills gaps with a plausible story rather than the frame, and garbles read text: `"global flight safety following financial firm announcements"` versus reference `"global flights to safety following financial firm bankruptcy announcements"`.

### reels-vision
1. Misses verbatim on-screen strings. `"The email's subject line is 'Inquiry Regarding Fully Funded PhD Opportunities in Machine Learning, Financial Distress and Firm Performance'."`
2. Adds advice rewrites: `"Utilize anime.js to add dynamic animations to your websites"`. Grounded — anime.js and its pitch are in the reference — but restated as instruction to the viewer, not as a claim about the reel.
3. Instruction following. It gets the five-item structure right (`"SAD Servers, The Testing Trophy, Google's Code Review Guide, MIT's Missing Semester"`) but reformats claims into imperatives, so nothing matches.

### qwen3vl-2b
1. Misses everything claim-shaped: `only_candidate` is `[]` on all three reels, and on the vibe-coder reel `candidate_summary` is `""`.
2. Adds nothing. Nothing to quote — no `only_candidate` entry exists.
3. Context length / capacity. Its two non-empty summaries are unanchored generalities (`"Key steps include checking if the ball is within the court boundaries"` — not in the reference), and the longest reel produced empty output entirely.

### minicpm-v45
1. Misses caption-level claims. `"The reel is a paid partnership with Manus, tagged #manuspartner and #ad in the caption."`
2. Adds frame-anchored detail: `"bklit UI provides beautiful charts such as bar charts, pie charts, and line graphs (frame 8 at 16s)."` Grounded — reference covers bklit's charts.
3. Instruction following. It anchors hard to frames (`"(frame 3, 6s)"`, `"(frame 16, 32s)"`) and so never reaches caption or hashtag material, which lives outside the frames.

### gemma4-12b
1. Misses small-print on-screen text. `"The email is addressed to 'Dr Tavoshianis' at Southampton Business School, University of Southampton, and carries the banner 'CAUTION: This e-mail originated outside the University of Southampton.'"`
2. Adds interpretation: `"The email shown serves as an example of a high-quality student outreach."` Grounded — reference says the academic `"explains why it worked"` — but abstract, so it matches nothing verbatim.
3. Resolution of on-screen text. Large overlays land (`"the creator recommends five specific resources: sadservers, the testing trophy..."`); the fine-print email header and publication list do not.

### What the evidence supports

Supported: qwen3vl-2b is unusable here — `only_candidate: []` on all three reels and `candidate_summary: ""` on one. local and reels-vision emit strings, not claims (`"THAT MEANS"`, `"BEFORE"`), so they need a post-step before their output is comparable. minicpm-v45 and qwen3vl-8b produce the most claim-shaped output and recover real detail (`"bklit UI provides beautiful charts..."`, `"HackerRank states on X.com that 'LeetCode is dead'"`). qwen3vl-4b carries a correctness risk the others do not show: `"Touching the kitchen area is now considered illegal under the 2025 rules."`

Not supported: any accuracy ranking. `reference` is itself one model's extraction, unverified against the videos — so `only_candidate` entries like `"@hackerrank tweeted that LeetCode is dead"` may be right and merely absent from reference. Three reels, one run each, no per-claim scores, no cost or latency. The local/reels-vision identical output is unexplained by these examples.
